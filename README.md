## Project Summary

MeryenDash: Vendor Locator Platform is a proposed digital marketplace designed to help customers find nearby street food vendors, roaming vendors, and small-scale food sellers within their local community. Vendors can create a profile, provide information about their food offerings, share their current location, and update their availability. Customers can search for nearby vendors, view their location and food information, check availability, and contact vendors. The platform aims to increase vendor visibility while making food discovery faster and more convenient for customers.
